# Product roadmap

The dashboard serves readers who want to trace a drug research update back to its source. The first release supports ingredient search, three research questions, observed weekly changes and selected safety readings.

## Next milestones

- Add jurisdiction-specific comparisons with a separate row per ingredient, brand, formulation, indication and regulator. Link each claim to the local label and revision date.
- Link publications to registry records using explicit trial identifiers.
- Evaluate whether readers can find a trial with posted results, identify its study population and locate the original evidence. Record completion time and errors.
